Young African Internet was founded with a simple belief: internet access should be more affordable, dependable and accessible to the communities that need it. This first update explains who we are, what we are building and where things stand today.

## Who we are

Young African Internet is an African-founded connectivity initiative being established in Uganda. The company is founded by Mr Henry Kasozi and Mr Obadiah Incarnate, who share an interest in using technology and connectivity to create practical opportunities for people, families and businesses.

Our mission is to expand access to affordable, dependable internet and help communities take part in education, communication, business and the digital economy. Our tagline says the same thing in four words: Connecting Communities. Empowering Generations.

## What we plan to offer

We are focusing on three things:

- **Cheap internet.** Affordable options for everyday use, with the price, duration, allowance and renewal steps stated clearly for every package.
- **A quality standard network.** A network built for dependable connectivity, with published coverage areas, support hours and notices about maintenance.
- **Gift cards for consistent clients.** An appreciation programme planned to reward eligible loyal customers, with clear rules for who qualifies.

Community Wi-Fi hotspots are a practical way to start. One good connection, shared through a hotspot at a shop, school or community venue, can bring the cost of getting online down for everyone nearby. Visitors buy a voucher and connect from their own phones, with no contract and no router at home.

## Where things stand

We are preparing our first service areas. Our packages and prices are now published: from UGX 500 for 3 hours and UGX 1,000 for 24 hours, up to UGX 25,000 for 30 days or UGX 45,000 for 30 days on three devices at once. When an area goes live, we will list it on the coverage page. Until then, we will not suggest that service is available anywhere it is not.

## How you can help

Tell us where you need internet access. Use the coverage page on our website to send your village, zone or nearest landmark. If you run a shop, school, clinic or community venue and would like to host a hotspot, contact us through the partners page.

You can also email us at younginvestorschoice8@gmail.com. We read every message.
